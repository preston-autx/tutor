# Tutor — Reinventing Calculus from Scratch

A living course for going from **Algebra 2 / Trig → AP Calculus**, built on one rule:

> We never *memorize* a formula. We *rebuild* it, so that if you forgot it on a desert
> island you could derive it again with a stick in the sand.

## How this works
- One idea at a time, taught until it's intuition, not memory.
- Every formula gets **derived** in front of you. No "trust me."
- Pictures first, symbols second.
- After the basics are solid, we go hunting for clever shortcuts — and *prove* them.

## The map (where we're going)
Calculus is the study of two things, which turn out to be the same thing flipped around:

1. **Derivatives** — how fast something is changing *right now* (slope of a curve at a point).
2. **Integrals** — how much has *accumulated* (area under a curve).
3. **The Fundamental Theorem** — the punchline: #1 and #2 are inverses of each other.

Everything else (limits, rules, trig derivatives, optimization, related rates) is
machinery in service of those three ideas.
